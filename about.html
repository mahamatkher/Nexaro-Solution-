About Nexaro Solutions Group

A premier technology and business-services company based in Chad, dedicated to connecting solutions and creating possibilities across Africa.

Our Story

Empowering Businesses Through Innovation

Nexaro Solutions Group was founded with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between complex technological challenges and practical, scalable business solutions. Operating from the heart of Chad, we understand the unique landscape of the African market.

Whether it's custom software development, secure digital payments, advanced CCTV infrastructure, or comprehensive travel and visa assistance, we bring deep expertise and a customer-first approach to every project. We don't just provide services; we build lasting partnerships designed to propel your operations forward.

To deliver innovative, reliable, and tailored solutions in technology, digital services, security, and travel that simplify operations and drive exponential growth for our clients. We strive to be the catalyst for digital transformation in every business we touch.
